## 1. Scope

**Covered (built so far):**
- header, menu drawer and announcement bar
- home page: hero, bestsellers, occasions, story, reviews, promise, FAQ, newsletter
- footer
- search panel and search results page
- cart drawer, cart page, and the offers in them (free shipping, free gift)
- Saved page and the hearts
- Track order page
- account page, the signed-in header menu, and the sign-in sheet

**Left out on purpose (upcoming phases):** SEO, checkout and payments, the product page, and the pages still on starter markup (collections list, contact, blog, 404, password, gift card). The first version of this audit included them; those findings were removed on 2026-10-04 and will be looked at again when each phase is built.

## 2. Summary

- **The existing gates pass.** `shopify theme check` is clean (101 files), `npm run check` passes 179 of 179, and the page crawl (`npm run audit`, Chrome) reports no script errors, no Liquid errors and no accessibility violations.
- **25 issues found** in the built parts, none of which those gates look for:
  - 10 bugs in behaviour (section 4)
  - 9 layout and text issues, most at large text or on 320 to 360px phones (section 5)
  - 6 other items (section 6)
- **The four that matter most:**
  1. The free gift shows up as something to shop: a "Free gift" chip in Popular searches, and a listing behind every "Shop all" link (B1).
  2. With a larger browser text size, the desktop header pushes Cart off the screen and every page scrolls sideways (L1). On a phone at 200% text the cart button cannot be reached and cart prices are cut off (L2).
  3. When the network drops, the cart shows "Failed to fetch" and a quantity that was never saved (B2).
  4. Hero photo labels are cut short on 320 to 360px phones at normal text size (L3).

| L3 | Hero photo labels | The name on the photo is cut to make room for the price at normal text size: "Sunflower b…" at 320px, and "Flower hair clips" loses its end at 360px, the most common Android width. **Checked and fine:**
- No page scrolls sideways at any of the nine widths at normal text size.
- Long product names, long prices and a 99+ cart count fit on cards, in the grids and in the footer.
- The menu drawer, search panel, cart drawer, price details, sign-in sheet, account and Track pages have nothing past the edge at 320px, at 360px, or at 130% text.
- Typed search text is escaped, and a long recent search wraps instead of running off.
