# Size sheet for Priya

Part of `docs/seo-plan.md`, phase 3. These pieces have no size on their page yet. Shoppers ask for it, and Google shows it.
Please measure one finished piece of each and write the numbers in. Centimetres are enough; I add inches.

- **Bouquets:** height and width, wrap included.
- **Keychains and charms:** the crochet piece alone (height × width), and the full length with ring and chain.
- **Flower pots:** height with the flower, and the width of the pot.
- **Hair pieces:** the flower's width; for headbands, the length of the flower row and of the whole cord.
- **Tie-back:** the flower's width and the length around the curtain.

When this comes back, Claude adds the sizes to `tools/catalog/catalog.json` and sends them to the store.
