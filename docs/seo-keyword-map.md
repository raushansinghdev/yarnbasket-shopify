# Keyword map: which page owns which search

Part of `docs/seo-plan.md` (phase 0.1). One page owns each search; no two pages chase the same one. Every title, heading, intro and link in the later phases is written from this table.

**Where the searches come from:** Google's own suggestions for India (`gl=in`, 44 seed searches, pulled 2026-10-12; raw list in the session scratchpad). Suggestions are what people really type, in rough order of popularity, but they carry no numbers. **Volumes are still to add** from Google Ads Keyword Planner (Raushan opens the account; then the "Volume" column is filled and the order of work follows it).

## What the suggestions tell us

1. **"Price" is the commonest second word.** "crochet bouquet price", "crochet sunflower keychain price in india", "crochet flower pot price", "crochet scrunchies price in india". Shoppers want the price before they click. So: the price goes in the product title tag's description, in the structured data (so Google prints it), and each collection's text states its price range in plain words.
2. **"Under ₹…" searches are real:** "crochet flower bouquet under 200", "crochet keychain under 50 / under 100", "woolen flower bouquet under 100", "evil eye crochet keychain under 100". Price-band pages are worth having, and their names should say the amount.
3. **India says "woolen" and "wool" as well as "crochet":** "woolen flower bouquet", "wool flower bouquet price", "wool keychain handmade". These words must appear in our text (never in place of "crochet", beside it).
4. **Who it is for:** "crochet gifts for men / women / teachers / sister / brother / boyfriend / girlfriend / best friend", "crochet flower bouquet for boyfriend / birthday / wedding / graduation", "crochet gift for friendship day", "crochet return gifts for kids", "crochet wedding return gifts".
5. **Pieces we sell that people search by name:** "crochet sunflower pot", "crochet tulip keychain", "crochet lily of the valley keychain / bag charm", "crochet daisy keychain", "crochet rose bouquet", "crochet tulip hair tie", "crochet hair clips for girls", "crochet curtain tie backs".
6. **Questions:** "how to wash crochet flowers", "how to wash crochet keychain", "do crochet flowers die", "can crochet flowers be washed", "how much do crochet flowers cost", "do girls like crochet flowers". Each is a guide or an FAQ answer.
7. **"Meesho" and "near me" follow many searches.** We cannot own "near me" (no shop to visit); "delivered across India" in titles and text answers the same need. People looking for our pieces on Meesho are already our buyers: the brand name on every page lets them find the site.
8. **Most "crochet" searches are from people who crochet** (pattern, tutorial, free pattern, for beginners). We do not chase those; the guides can catch a few ("how a crochet bouquet is made").
9. **Seen in suggestions, not in our catalogue:** crochet rakhi, gajra and Navratri hair pieces, bookmarks, bracelets, tops and bags. A note for Priya on what people look for, not a promise to make them.

## Rules that follow from the map

- **Title tag:** the main search first, in natural words, then "| Yarn Basket". Product titles keep the piece's name.
- **Description:** the main search once, the price or price range, "made by hand in India", delivery.
- **h1:** the page's plain name. **Intro:** the main search in the first sentence, "woolen" or "wool" once where it reads naturally.
- **One page per search.** If two pages start to rank for the same search, the weaker one links to the stronger with that search as the link's words.
- **New pages are built only when 6 or more pieces fit** (the plan's choice 4). Counts are checked in phase 4 before any page is created.
