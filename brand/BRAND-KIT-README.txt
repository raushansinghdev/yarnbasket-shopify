YARN BASKET · BRAND KIT
=======================

Final logo: the 2A "bouquet basket" (basket with a yarn ball, crochet hook and crochet flower).
Brand colour: Blush.

PNG = ready to use anywhere (transparent background unless it has a coloured background by design).
SVG = vector version for printers, sticker shops and designers. Scales to any size without blur.
      The text is already converted to shapes, so no fonts need to be installed.

1-logo/
  logo-stacked-colour        Main logo. Website header, packaging, posts. (3000 px wide)
  logo-stacked-reverse       Same, for dark backgrounds and photos.
  logo-horizontal-colour     For wide, short spaces: website bar, labels, email signature.
  logo-horizontal-reverse    Same, for dark backgrounds.
  icon-colour                Just the basket. Favicon, small stamps, watermark. (2000 × 2000)
  icon-reverse               Same, for dark backgrounds.
  icon-one-colour            Line-only version for rubber stamps, embossing, single-ink printing.
  wordmark-colour / reverse  Name and tagline only.

2-instagram/
  instagram-profile-1080     Profile picture. Upload as is; Instagram crops it to a circle.
                             Also works wherever a shop asks for a square logo or profile image.
  highlight-*.png            Story highlight covers (1080 × 1920): Bouquets, Keychains,
                             Hair clips, Reviews, How to order.
                             To use: post the image to your story, then add it as the highlight cover.

3-banner/
  banner-1920x600            Website / store header, Facebook cover. If a platform asks for a
                             different size, ask for a resized version.

4-stickers/
  sticker-logo               Round logo sticker, 3000 × 3000. Print at 2–3 inch (5–7.5 cm).
  sticker-thank-you          "Thank you for shopping handmade" sticker for parcels.
                             Send the SVG to the printer if they accept it; otherwise the PNG.

5-animation/
  logo-animation-1080x1080.mp4       Square video (cream). Instagram post, WhatsApp status.
  logo-animation-1080x1920-story.mp4 Vertical video (blush). Reels intro/outro, stories.
  logo-animation.gif                 Small looping GIF for emails and chats.
  logo-animation.html                Open in a browser to watch it; has Replay and background buttons.
  website-splash-snippet.html        Intro splash for your website. Plays the animation once
                                     when someone opens the site, then fades away.
                                     Setup instructions are at the top of the file
                                     (Shopify: paste right after <body> in layout/theme.liquid).

COLOURS
  Cocoa  #6B4F43   lines, the name, text
  Blush  #F2D4CC   brand background, Instagram
  Rose   #E3A69C   yarn, accents on blush
  Sage   #A9B8A0   leaf, small highlights
  Oat    #F5EFE6   soft page background
  Cream  #FBF6EF   basket, splash background
  Taupe  #8E7468   tagline

FONTS (free on Google Fonts)
  Cormorant Garamond Italic, SemiBold   for the name and headings
  Jost Medium, capitals, wide spacing   for the tagline and small labels

USING THE LOGO
  · Leave empty space around it at least as wide as the yarn ball.
  · Smallest sizes: icon 24 px on screen / 1 cm in print; stacked logo 120 px / 3 cm.
  · Don't stretch it, rotate it, change its colours, or add shadows or outlines.
